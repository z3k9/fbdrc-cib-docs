---
title: "Settings"
description: "General platform settings and preferences"
---

Settings is where you manage your personal profile, security, company details, and how the platform looks.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Profile">
    Shows your **Personal Information** (Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Account Status) and your **Organisation Information** (Organisation Name, Type, Email, Phone, whether you're an Officer, Customer ID, and Auth Count).

    <Frame caption="Profile: personal and organisation information">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/settings/settings-1.png" alt="Settings Profile tab showing Personal Information with Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Account Status, and Organisation Information with Organisation Name, Type, Email, Phone, Officer status, and Customer ID" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Security">
    Update your account password. You'll need to log in again after changing it.

    <Frame caption="Security: change password">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/settings/settings-2.png" alt="Settings Security tab, Change Password form with Current Password, New Password, and Confirm New Password fields, and an Update Password button" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Company Details">
    Shows registered company information: Organisation Name, Type, Incorporation Number and Date, Country, State, City, Street, Organisation Email/Phone, Website, and Transaction Limit.

    <Frame caption="Company Details: registered company information">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/settings/settings-3.png" alt="Settings Company Details tab showing Organisation Name, Organisation Type, Inc. Number, Inc. Date, Country, State, City, Street, Organisation Email, Organisation Phone, Website, and Transaction Limit fields" />
    </Frame>

    <Note>
    Several fields here (Inc. Number, Inc. Date, Country, State, City, Street, Website, Transaction Limit) may show as blank until populated for your organisation.
    </Note>
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Appearance">
    Personalize how the platform looks. This mirrors the quick Appearance panel available from the top bar, but as a full settings page.

    <Frame caption="Appearance: theme, colour, density, text size, and background">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/settings/settings-4.png" alt="Settings Appearance tab with Theme options Light, Dark, Dim, and System, Accent Colour swatches, Density options Roomy, Cozy, and Compact, Text Size options, Background options, and a Reset to defaults link" />
    </Frame>

    | Setting | Options |
    |---|---|
    | **Theme** | Light, Dark, Dim, System |
    | **Accent Colour** | FBN Navy (default) plus 5 alternates |
    | **Density** | Roomy, Cozy, Compact |
    | **Text Size** | Default, Comfortable, Large |
    | **Background** | None, Mesh, Dots, Grid, Custom |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Info>
A **Corporate Documents** tab also appears in the Settings sidebar but hasn't been captured yet.
</Info>
