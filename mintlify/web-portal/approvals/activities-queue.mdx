The Activities Queue tracks administrative requests (such as limit changes and user-organisation links) that require approval before taking effect.

</Frame>

## Summary cards

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Requests awaiting approval |
| **Approved** | Requests approved so far |
| **Rejected** | Requests that were rejected |

## Activities Queue table

Each row shows the **Module** the request applies to (e.g. `ORG_LIMIT`, `OFFICER_LIMIT`, `USER_ORG_LINK`), the **Action** taken (e.g. `UPDATE`, `LINK`), who **Initiated** it, its current **Status** (e.g. `BANKPROCESSING`, `BANKAPPROVED`), who **Approved** it, and when it was **Submitted**. Rows can be bulk-selected via checkboxes, and each has a view action for full detail.
