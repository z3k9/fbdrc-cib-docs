---
title: "Officers"
description: "Add and manage the people who operate your organisation's account"
---
Officers are managed under **Administration → Officers**. This is where a company adds the people who will actually operate the account (Initiators, Authorizers, or a combined role) and where their identity and contact details are captured for the bank's records.

<Frame caption="Officers List: every officer added to the organisation, with role, verification, and status">
  <img src="/images/web-portal/officers/officers-1.png" alt="Officers List screen showing columns for S/N, First Name, Last Name, Role, Email, Phone, Bank Verified, Status, Active Status, and Actions, with an Add Officer button" />
</Frame>

The list shows each officer's **Role** (e.g. Role Authorizer, Role Initiator, or a combined Role Initiator Authorizer), whether they're **Bank Verified**, and their current **Status**: Inactive, Active, Logged In, or New.

## Adding an officer

Selecting **Add Officer** opens a 5-step wizard:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bio Data">
    First Name, Last Name, Middle Name, Mother's Maiden Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Nationality, and Designation.

    <Frame caption="Add Officer: Step 1: Bio Data">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/officers/officers-2.png" alt="Add Officer wizard step 1, Bio Data, with fields for First Name, Last Name, Middle Name, Mother's Maiden Name, Gender, Date of Birth, Nationality, and Designation" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Contact">
    Country, State, City, Street Address, Phone (with country code), Other Phone Number, Email, and Username.

    <Frame caption="Add Officer: Step 2: Contact">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/officers/officers-3.png" alt="Add Officer wizard step 2, Contact, with fields for Country, State, City, Street Address, Phone, Other Phone Number, Email, and Username" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Identification">
    Means of Identification (e.g. National ID Card), ID Number, Issue Date, and Expiry Date.

    <Frame caption="Add Officer: Step 3: Identification">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/officers/officers-4.png" alt="Add Officer wizard step 3, Identification, with fields for Means of Identification, ID Number, Issue Date, and Expiry Date" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Employer">
    Captures the officer's employer details for the bank's records.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Role">
    Role (selected from a list, e.g. `ROLE_INITIATOR`) and Class of Signatory (e.g. `A`). Selecting **Add Officer** completes the wizard.

    <Frame caption="Add Officer: Step 5: Role">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/officers/officers-5.png" alt="Add Officer wizard step 5, Role, with a Role dropdown showing ROLE_INITIATOR and a Class of Signatory field, and an Add Officer button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Officers can only log in once First Bank DRC has approved them and verified their bank details. Login credentials are sent directly to each officer once approved.
</Note>
