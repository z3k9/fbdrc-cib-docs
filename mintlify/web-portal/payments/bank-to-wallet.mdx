---
title: "Bank to Wallet"
description: "Fund mobile wallets directly from your bank account"
---

Bank to Wallet sends funds from your account directly to a mobile money wallet (e.g. Airtel Money, M-Pesa).

## The transfer flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Recipient">
    Choose the source account and enter the recipient's wallet details, including the wallet **Provider** and wallet number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount">
    Enter the amount and an optional **Narration**. VAT and Commission are calculated automatically, shown alongside the **Total Debit**.

    <Frame caption="Enter Amount: with VAT, Commission, and Total Debit breakdown">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/bank-to-wallet/bank-to-wallet-1.png" alt="Bank to Wallet step 2, Enter Amount, showing the amount, available balance, Narration field, and a breakdown of VAT, Commission, and Total Debit" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Review the From account, To (Wallet) recipient and number, Provider (e.g. Airtel), Amount, VAT, Commission, Total Debit, and Narration, then select **Submit Transfer**.

    <Frame caption="Review Transfer before submitting">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/bank-to-wallet/bank-to-wallet-2.png" alt="Bank to Wallet step 3, Review Transfer, showing From, To (Wallet), Provider, Amount, VAT, Commission, Total Debit, and Narration, with a Submit Transfer button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The transfer is submitted successfully.

    <Frame caption="Transfer Submitted confirmation">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/bank-to-wallet/bank-to-wallet-3.png" alt="Transfer Submitted confirmation showing the amount and recipient, with New Transfer and View Transactions buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
