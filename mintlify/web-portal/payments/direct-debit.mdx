---
title: "Direct Debit"
description: "Stay in control of recurring payment mandates on your accounts"
---

Direct Debit sets up a recurring payment mandate against another organisation's account, with their approval required before it becomes active.

<Frame caption="Finding the organisation to raise a direct debit against">
  <img src="/images/web-portal/direct-debit/direct-debit-1.png" alt="Direct Debit page, step 1, Find Organisation, with a search field asking who you would like to raise a direct debit against" />
</Frame>

## Setting up a direct debit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Organisation">
    Search for the organisation the mandate should apply to.

    <Frame caption="Search results with Raise Direct Debit action">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/direct-debit/direct-debit-2.png" alt="Find Organisation search results showing a matching organisation with Email and Country columns, and Raise Direct Debit and More action buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mandate Details">
    Set the **Credit Account** (with available balance shown), **Amount**, **Frequency**, **Day of Week**, and **Start/Expiry Date**, plus a **Narration**.

    <Frame caption="Setting up the direct debit mandate">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/direct-debit/direct-debit-3.png" alt="Set up your direct debit mandate form with fields for Credit Account, Amount, Frequency, Day of Week, Start Date, Expiry Date, and Narration, with a Raise Direct Debit button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The mandate is submitted for approval from both parties. Once approved, it runs on the configured schedule.

    <Frame caption="Direct Debit Submitted: awaiting approval">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/direct-debit/direct-debit-4.png" alt="Direct Debit Submitted confirmation, Awaiting Approval, showing the amount and organisation, with New Direct Debit and View Approvals buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
