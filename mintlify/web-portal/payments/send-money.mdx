Send Money is the standard way to transfer funds to any bank account, whether a saved beneficiary or a new recipient.

<Frame caption="Select Recipient: source account, saved beneficiaries, or a new recipient">
  <img src="/images/web-portal/send-money/send-money-1.png" alt="Send Money step 1, Select Recipient, with Source Account dropdown, Saved Beneficiary and New Recipient tabs, a beneficiary search field, Add Beneficiary link, and a list of beneficiaries tagged Intrabank or Interbank" />
</Frame>

## The transfer flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Recipient">
    Select the **Source Account**, then choose a **Saved Beneficiary** (searchable list, each tagged Intrabank or Interbank) or enter a **New Recipient**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount">
    Enter the amount. Choose a **Payment Channel**, either **ACH** (standard interbank transfer) or **RTGS** (real-time settlement for large transfers), and add an optional **Narration**.

    <Frame caption="Enter Amount: payment channel, amount, and narration">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/send-money/send-money-2.png" alt="Send Money step 2, Enter Amount, showing You Send and They Receive currency, Payment Channel options ACH and RTGS, an amount field, Beneficiary receives amount, and a Narration field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review & Confirm">
    Review the full transfer summary (Initiated By, Source and Destination Account details, Payment Channel, Amount, and Narration), then select **Confirm Transfer**.

    <Frame caption="Review Transfer: full summary before confirming">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/send-money/send-money-3.png" alt="Send Money step 3, Review Transfer, showing Initiated By, Source Account Number and Name, Destination Account Name and Number, Destination Bank, Payment Channel, Amount, and Narration, with a Confirm Transfer button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The transaction is pushed to the Transaction Queue, awaiting approval from your company's Authorizer, with a Batch Reference for tracking.

    <Frame caption="Transaction Pushed to Queue: awaiting approval">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/send-money/send-money-4.png" alt="Send Money step 4, Done, showing Transaction Pushed to Queue, Awaiting Approval, the amount and recipient, a Batch Reference, and New Transfer and View Transaction Queue buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
An **Upload in bulk** shortcut is available from the Send Money screen, linking through to Bulk Transfer for multiple payments at once.
</Note>
