FCY Interbank Transfer handles foreign currency payments to other banks, with support for both saved beneficiaries and one-time transfers.

## Transfer details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Debit Account & Payment Order Type">
    Select the account to debit and the payment order type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Beneficiary">
    Choose **Saved Beneficiary** (select from your list, or **Add Beneficiary**) or **One-time Transfer** for a beneficiary you won't reuse. Beneficiary Address is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount & Currency">
    Enter the **Amount** and select the **Transfer Currency**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reference & Reason">
    Add an optional **Payment Reference** and a required **Transaction Reason**, then select **Next**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
This module continues to a **Summary** step and a **Done** confirmation, following the same pattern as other transfer types. Screenshots of those steps haven't been captured yet.
</Info>
