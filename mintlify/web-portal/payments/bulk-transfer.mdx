Bulk Transfer handles batch payments across **all** transfer types (Intrabank, Interbank, Foreign Currency, and Bank to Wallet) in one module, selected via tabs.

## Setting up a bulk transfer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Transfer Type">
    Select the tab matching the type of transfer: **Intrabank**, **Interbank**, **Foreign Currency**, or **Bank to Wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Debit Mode">
    **Single Debit** charges the total in one go; **Multiple Debit** charges per transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download a Template">
    Use the **Excel**, **CSV**, or **TXT** template to prepare your transfer file in the expected format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload and Submit">
    Drop your completed file (`.xlsx` or `.csv`) into the upload zone, then select **Validate & Submit**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Batch history

Every submitted batch appears in the Batch History table, showing Batch ID, Name, Status (e.g. `INITIATED - TRAN`, `PROCESSED - NA`), Total/Valid/Invalid/Initiated counts, who created it, and the date.
