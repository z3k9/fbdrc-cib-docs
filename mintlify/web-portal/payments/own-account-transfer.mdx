Own Account Transfer moves funds between accounts your organisation already holds. No beneficiary setup needed.

<Frame caption="Select Accounts: choose source and destination">
  <img src="/images/web-portal/own-account-transfer/own-account-transfer-1.png" alt="Own Account Transfer step 1, Select Accounts, with From Account and To Account dropdowns, each showing the account and its available balance" />
</Frame>

## The transfer flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accounts">
    Select the **From Account** and **To Account**. Each shows its available balance once selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount">
    Enter the amount to move and an optional **Narration**.

    <Frame caption="Amount and narration">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/own-account-transfer/own-account-transfer-2.png" alt="Own Account Transfer step 2, Amount, showing the amount entered, available balance, and a Narration field" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Review the From account, To account, Amount, and Narration, then select **Confirm Transfer**.

    <Frame caption="Review Transfer before confirming">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/own-account-transfer/own-account-transfer-3.png" alt="Own Account Transfer step 3, Review Transfer, showing From, To, Amount, and Narration, with a Confirm Transfer button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    <Info>
    A confirmation screen appears once submitted, following the same pattern as other transfer types in this platform (transaction pushed to queue, awaiting approval). A screenshot of this specific screen hasn't been captured yet.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>
