Invoices lets you raise invoices against other organisations on the platform and manage what's been sent to or received from them.

  <img src="/images/web-portal/invoices/invoices-1.png" alt="Invoices page with Sent Invoices and Received Invoices tabs, and step 1, Find Organisation, with a search field asking who you would like to raise an invoice against" />
</Frame>

## Raising an invoice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find Organisation">
    Search for the organisation you want to invoice by name.

    <Frame caption="Search results with Raise Invoice action">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/invoices/invoices-2.png" alt="Find Organisation search results showing a matching organisation with Type, Email, and Count columns, and Raise Invoice and More action buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invoice Details">
    Enter the invoice **Name**, **Reference No.**, **Credit Account** (with available balance shown), **Amount**, **Currency**, **Due Date**, and **Narration**.

    <Frame caption="Entering invoice details">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/invoices/invoices-3.png" alt="Invoice Details form with fields for Name, Reference No., Credit Account, Amount, Currency, Due Date, and Narration, and a Raise Invoice button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The invoice is submitted for approval from both parties, and will process on its due date once approved.

    <Frame caption="Invoice Submitted: awaiting approval">
      <img src="/images/web-portal/invoices/invoices-4.png" alt="Invoice Submitted confirmation, Awaiting Approval, showing the amount and organisation, with New Invoice and View Approvals buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
