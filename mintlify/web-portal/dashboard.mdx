The Dashboard is the first screen you land on after signing in. It gives a portfolio-level view of your accounts, cash flow, and anything waiting for your attention.

## What's on the Dashboard

- **FX Rates ticker**: Friday's FX Rates for USD/CDF, EUR/CDF, GBP/CDF, and ZAR/CDF
- **Current Balance**: toggle between USD and CDF, with a show/hide control for privacy
- **Monthly Inflow / Monthly Outflow**: this month's totals at a glance
- **Pending Approvals**: a count of items requiring your action
- **Cash Flow chart**: inflow vs. outflow over time, toggleable between USD/CDF and adjustable time ranges (e.g. last 6 months)
- **Quick Actions**: one-click shortcuts to Send Money, Pay Bills, Bulk Transfer, Beneficiaries, Accounts, Invoices, Statements, Direct Debit, FCY Interbank Transfer, and Bank to Wallet
- **Recent Transactions**: a short list with a "View all" link through to the full Transactions module
- **Pending Approvals list**: the specific items awaiting sign-off (invoices, uploads, etc.), with its own "View all" link

## Switching organisations

If you belong to more than one organisation on the platform, the organisation name in the top bar (e.g. "Premium Foods") is a dropdown. Selecting it lets you switch between organisations, each shown with its account number.

## Appearance settings

The sliders icon in the top bar opens an Appearance panel where you can personalize how the platform looks:

<Frame caption="Appearance panel: theme, accent colour, density, text size, and background">
  <img src="/images/web-portal/dashboard/dashboard-4.png" alt="Appearance settings panel with Theme options (Light, Dark, Auto, System), Accent Colour swatches including FBN Navy, Density options (Roomy, Cozy, Compact), Text Size options (Default, Comfortable, Large), and Background options (None, Mesh, Dots, Grid, Custom)" />
</Frame>

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | Light, Dark, Auto, System |
| **Accent Colour** | FBN Navy (default) plus 5 alternate colours |
| **Density** | Roomy, Cozy, Compact |
| **Text Size** | Default, Comfortable, Large |
| **Background** | None, Mesh, Dots, Grid, Custom |
