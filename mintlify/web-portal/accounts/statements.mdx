The Statements page lets you request a statement for a specific account and date range, either as a direct download or sent to your email.

<Frame caption="Request Statement form">
  <img src="/images/web-portal/statements/statements-1.png" alt="Request Statement form with fields for Account Number, From Date, To Date, and Format, and Download Statement and Send to Email buttons" />
</Frame>

## Requesting a statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Account Number">
    Choose the account you want a statement for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set From Date and To Date">
    Define the date range the statement should cover.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Format">
    Choose your preferred statement format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download or Send">
    Select **Download Statement** to save it directly, or **Send to Email** to have it delivered to your registered email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>
