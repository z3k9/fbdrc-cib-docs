Sweep Payments automate the movement of funds between accounts on a recurring basis.

## Creating a sweep order

Select **New Sweep Order** to set up a new automated transfer between two accounts, specifying the amount type, amount, and frequency (e.g. Daily, Weekly, Hourly).

## Sweep orders table

Each row shows the Reference, From Account, To Account, Amount Type (e.g. Flat), Amount, Frequency, and current Status, such as Pending or Submitted.
