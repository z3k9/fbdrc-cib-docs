The Cheque Management page is where you request new cheque books and track the status of existing requests.

## Requesting a cheque book

Select **Request Cheque Book** to submit a new request. Each request specifies the number of booklets and leaves needed.

## Tracking requests

The Cheque Requests table shows every request made, along with its current **Status**, such as Requested (awaiting approval) or OrgApproved (approved, with an Approval Date shown).
