The Accounts page gives you a consolidated view of every account your organisation holds, with balances broken down by currency.

## Summary cards

| Card | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Total Available Balance** | Combined available balance across all accounts, with account count |
| **Total Book Balance** | Combined book balance across all accounts |
| **CDF Balance** | Total balance held in Congolese Franc accounts |
| **USD Balance** | Total balance held in US Dollar accounts |

## My Accounts table

Each row shows Account Number, Account Name, Type (e.g. Current), Currency, Available Balance, Book Balance, and Status (e.g. Active). Use the view icon in the Actions column to see more detail on a specific account.

<Note>
The list view can be toggled between a table layout and a card layout using the view icons above the table. Refresh, filter, and download controls are also available.
</Note>
