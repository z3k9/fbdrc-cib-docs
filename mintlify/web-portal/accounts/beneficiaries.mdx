The Beneficiaries page stores your saved payment recipients, so you don't need to re-enter their details every time you send money.

## Filtering beneficiaries

Beneficiaries can be filtered by category:

| Filter | Covers |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every saved beneficiary |
| **First Bank RDC** | Beneficiaries holding First Bank DRC accounts |
| **Other Banks - RDC** | Beneficiaries at other DRC-based banks |
| **Other Banks - Intl** | Beneficiaries at international banks |

## Managing beneficiaries

Select **Add Beneficiary** to save a new recipient. Each existing beneficiary can be edited or deleted directly from the list, or used immediately via the **Send Money** shortcut next to their entry.
