## Contacting First Bank DRC

| Channel | Details |
|---|---|
| **Phone Support** | Speak to a dedicated relationship manager, Mon to Fri, 8AM to 8PM WAT |
| **Email Support** | Send a detailed request, with a response within 4 hours |

## Guided tours

Step-by-step walkthroughs are available for every major section of the platform: Dashboard, Account Services, Approval Queue, Payment Services, Administration, Settings, and Support. Select **Start Tour** next to any section to launch it.

## Submitting a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Category and Priority">
    Choose the category and priority that best match your issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Subject and Message">
    Provide a brief subject line and a detailed description of the issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Select **Submit Ticket**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## My Tickets

Submitted tickets appear in a table showing Ticket No, Subject, Category, Priority, Status (Open, Resolved, Closed), Submitted and Last Updated dates, and the assigned officer.
