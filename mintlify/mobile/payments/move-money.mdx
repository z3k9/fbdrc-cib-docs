title: "Move money"

---

Select **Pay** in the bottom navigation bar to open **Move money**. It lists every way you can send funds from the app.

<Frame caption="The Move money menu">
  <img src="/images/mobile/pay.jpg" alt="Pay screen titled Move money, listing Transfer options (Move between own accounts, To local bank, Foreign Transfer FCY, Bank to Wallet, Pay bills), a Manage section with Beneficiaries, and Recent recipients" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
</Frame>

| Option | Use it to | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Move between own accounts** | Transfer between your organisation's own First Bank accounts | [Own account transfer](/mobile/payments/own-account-transfer) |
| **To local bank** | Pay an account at a bank in the DRC, such as FBNBank, Rawbank, Equity, or TMB | [Send money](/mobile/payments/send-money) |
| **Foreign Transfer (FCY)** | Make a foreign currency payment through SWIFT, to a bank in the DRC or abroad | [Foreign transfer](/mobile/payments/foreign-transfer) |
| **Bank to Wallet** | Fund a mobile money wallet | |
| **Pay bills** | Pay for power, internet, taxes, and more | |

Under **Manage**, **Beneficiaries** takes you to your saved recipients. **Recent recipients** lists people you've paid recently, so you can pay them again quickly.

<Note>
Every transfer you submit goes to the **Transaction Queue**. It is only processed after an Authorizer approves it. See [Approvals](/mobile/approvals).
</Note>
