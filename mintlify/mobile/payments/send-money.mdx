---
title: "Send money to a local bank"
sidebarTitle: "Send money"
description: "Pay an account at another bank in the DRC using ACH or RTGS"
---

Use **To local bank** to pay an account at another bank in the DRC. The flow has four steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose who you're paying">
    Choose **Saved** to pick an existing beneficiary, or **New recipient** to enter new details:

    - **Select transfer type**, for example **Interbank Transfer**
    - **Beneficiary Bank**
    - **Account number**
    - **Account name**

    Tick **Save as beneficiary** to reuse these details next time. Then select **Continue**.

    <Frame caption="Step 1: recipient details">
      <img src="/images/mobile/send-money-1.jpg" alt="Send money step 1 of 4, Who are you paying, with Saved and New recipient tabs, transfer type set to Interbank Transfer, Beneficiary Bank, Account number and Account name fields, a Save as beneficiary checkbox, and a Continue button" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the amount and payment channel">
    Confirm the **From** account. Choose a **Payment channel**:

    | Channel | Use for |
    |---|---|
    | **ACH** | Standard interbank transfers |
    | **RTGS** | Real-time settlement for large transfers |

    Enter the amount and a **Narration** (required), then select **Continue**.

    <Frame caption="Step 2: amount and payment channel">
      <img src="/images/mobile/send-money-2.jpg" alt="Send money step 2 of 4 showing From and To accounts, Payment channel options ACH and RTGS, an amount of CDF 2,000,000, quick amount buttons, a keypad, a Narration field, and a Continue button" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the payment">
    Check the initiator, source account, destination name, account and bank, payment channel, amount, and narration. Select **Confirm Transfer**.

    <Frame caption="Step 3: review the payment">
      <img src="/images/mobile/send-money-3.jpg" alt="Review payment step 3 of 4 listing Initiated by, Source account number and name, Destination name, account and bank, Payment Channel, Amount and Narration, with a Confirm Transfer button" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment queued">
    The payment is pushed to the Transaction Queue to wait for an Authorizer. You'll be notified when it is approved or rejected, or if more action is needed.

    Choose **View Transaction Queue**, **New Transfer**, or **Done**.

    <Frame caption="Step 4: transaction pushed to queue">
      <img src="/images/mobile/send-money-4.jpg" alt="Transaction Pushed to Queue screen marked Awaiting Approval, showing the amount and recipient, Batch Reference, Bank and Date, with View Transaction Queue, New Transfer and Done buttons" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
