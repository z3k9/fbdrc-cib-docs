---
title: "Own account transfer"
description: "Move funds between your organisation's own accounts"
---

Use **Move between own accounts** to move money between accounts your organisation holds with First Bank DRC. No beneficiary is needed. The transfer takes two steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose accounts and enter the amount">
    Pick the **From** and **To** accounts. Enter the amount with the keypad, or tap a quick amount such as **CDF10K** or **CDF50K**. If the two accounts are in different currencies, the app shows the converted amount and the current rate.

    Enter a **Narration** (required), then select **Move**.

    <Frame caption="Step 1: choose accounts and amount">
      <img src="/images/mobile/own-account-1.jpg" alt="Move between accounts step 1 of 2, with From and To account selectors, an amount entry showing CDF 20,000 with its USD equivalent and rate, quick amount buttons, a keypad, a Narration field, and a Move button" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and confirm">
    Check the summary: initiator, source and destination account numbers and names, amount, and narration. Then select **Confirm Transfer**.

    <Frame caption="Step 2: review the transfer">
      <img src="/images/mobile/own-account-2.jpg" alt="Review transfer step 2 of 2 showing the amount, Initiated by, From account and name, To account and name, Amount, Narration, a Rate subject to change notice, and a Confirm Transfer button" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
    For cross-currency transfers, the rate shown is not final. It is locked in only when the transfer is approved, so the final amount may shift slightly with the market.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Transfer submitted">
    The transfer is sent to the Transaction Queue to wait for approval. Note the **Batch Reference** if you need to track it.

    Select **View Transaction Queue** to follow it, or **Done** to finish.

    <Frame caption="Transfer submitted and awaiting approval">
      <img src="/images/mobile/own-account-3.jpg" alt="Transfer Submitted screen marked Awaiting Approval, showing Batch Reference, From, To, Amount and Date, with View Transaction Queue and Done buttons" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
