Use **Foreign Transfer (FCY)** to send a cross-border or foreign currency payment through SWIFT. You may need to attach supporting documents.

The same form handles two cases. You choose between them with **Where is the beneficiary bank?**

| Option | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Domestic** | You're sending foreign currency to a bank in the DRC |
| **International** | You're sending to a bank outside the DRC |

## Filling in the transfer details

<Steps>
  <Step title="Debit account and order type">
    Choose the **Debit account** and the **Payment order type**, for example **Goods**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Beneficiary bank location">
    Select **Domestic** or **International**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Beneficiary">
    Choose **Saved beneficiary** or **One-time recipient**. For a one-time recipient, enter:

    - **Beneficiary name** and **Beneficiary account number**
    - **Beneficiary country** (required)
    - **Beneficiary bank** (required)
    - **Beneficiary address** and **Beneficiary bank address**
    - **Bank BIC / SWIFT code** (optional, international transfers)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Amount and purpose">
    Enter the **Amount**. The app spells the amount out in words so you can check it. Add an optional **Reference**, a **Reason for transfer**, and the **Remittance purpose** (required), for example **Spare parts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Supporting documents">
    Upload the documents for the payment as PDF, JPG, or PNG files. The list includes **Invoice**, **Letter of undertaking**, **Custom Document**, **Inspection**, **Bill of Lading**, **Airway Bill**, and **Other**. Items labelled **Optional** can be skipped.

    Tick **Save as beneficiary** if you'll pay this recipient again, then select **Continue** to review and submit the transfer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Like every transfer, an FCY payment goes to the Transaction Queue and must be approved by an Authorizer before it is processed.
</Note>
