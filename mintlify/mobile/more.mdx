These features are under the **More** tab.

## Beneficiaries

Save the details of people and companies you pay often, so you don't have to enter them each time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Beneficiaries">
    Go to **More > Beneficiaries** and start a new beneficiary.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Choose the **bank**, then enter the **Account number** and **Account name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save beneficiary**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Saving a new beneficiary">
  <img src="/images/mobile/new-beneficiary.jpg" alt="New beneficiary screen titled Save a recipient, with a bank selector, Account number and Account name fields, and a Save beneficiary button" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
</Frame>

You can also save a beneficiary while making a payment by ticking **Save as beneficiary**.

## Statements

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the account">
    Go to **More > Statements** and select the account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Pick the **From** and **To** dates.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a format">
    Select the **Format**, for example **PDF**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the statement">
    Select **Download statement** to save it to your phone, or **Email statement** to have it sent to your registered email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  <img src="/images/mobile/statements.jpg" alt="Statements screen with an account selector, From and To date fields, a Format selector set to PDF, a confirmation that the PDF and SWIFT statements were sent by email, and Download statement and Email statement buttons" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
</Frame>

## Team

Go to **More > Team** to see the officers on your organisation's profile. Each entry shows the officer's name, email, role (**AUTHORIZER**, **INITIATOR**, or **INITIATOR_AUTHORIZER**), and when they were last active. A green dot means the officer is active now.

  <img src="/images/mobile/team.jpg" alt="Team screen listing officers with their initials, name, email (blurred), last active time and role badge" style={{ maxWidth: "300px" }} />
</Frame>

<Note>
To add officers or change their roles, use the Web Portal. See [Officers](/web-portal/administration/officers).
</Note>
