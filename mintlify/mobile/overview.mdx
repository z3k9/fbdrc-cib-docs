The CIB mobile app puts your corporate accounts in your pocket. Officers can check balances, initiate transfers, and approve items waiting in the queues, all from a phone.

The app is available in English and French, with light and dark themes.

## Finding your way around

A navigation bar sits at the bottom of every main screen:

| Tab | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Your dashboard of balances, FX rates, approvals, and recent activity |
| **Accounts** | The list of your organisation's accounts |
| **Pay** | The **Move money** menu for all transfer types |
| **Approvals** | The Transaction Queue and Activity Queue. A red badge shows how many items are waiting. |
| **More** | Your profile, Beneficiaries, Statements, Team, Settings, Support, Language, and **Sign out** |

## Before you start

<Check>You have an active officer profile on First Bank DRC CIB. See [Officers](/web-portal/administration/officers).</Check>
<Check>You know the email address and password for your CIB profile.</Check>

<Note>
Transfers you start on the mobile app follow the same approval rules as the Web Portal. They go to the Transaction Queue and must be approved by an Authorizer before the bank processes them.
</Note>
