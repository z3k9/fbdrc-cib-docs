Go to **More > Settings** to manage your own profile and app preferences.

## Profile

Select **Profile** to see your **Full name**, **Phone**, and **Email**. Your email address is used for sign-in and security alerts.

## Change password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Change password">
    Select **Change password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your passwords">
    Enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** twice. The new password must have at least 8 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number, and a symbol.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Update password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Biometrics

Turn on **Biometrics** to sign in and approve items with your phone's fingerprint or face recognition.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on the toggle">
    Switch on **Biometrics**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your password">
    Enter your password to confirm it's you, then select **Enable Biometrics**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Biometrics is set up for the device you're using.

## Dark mode

Switch on **Dark mode** to use the dark theme instead of following your phone's setting.

## Language

Select **Language** and choose **English** or **Français**. The app switches to the language you pick.
