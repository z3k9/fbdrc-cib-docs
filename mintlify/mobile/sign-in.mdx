## Signing in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your country">
    Open the app and confirm **Country** is set to **DRC**. You can change the app language with the flag at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter the **Email** and **Password** for your CIB profile. Use **Forgot?** in the password field if you need to reset it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Select **Sign in securely**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking your session

After you sign in, the app shows a short recap of the session:

| Detail | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Signed in as** | The officer name on the profile |
| **Device** | The phone model and operating system used for this session |
| **Last sign-in** | The date and time of your previous sign-in |

Select **Continue to dashboard** to go to [Home](/mobile/home).

<Warning>
If the device or last sign-in time doesn't look right, sign out straight away and contact your administrator.
</Warning>

<Tip>
After your first sign-in, you can turn on **Biometrics** to sign in and approve with your fingerprint or face. See [Settings](/mobile/settings#biometrics).
</Tip>
