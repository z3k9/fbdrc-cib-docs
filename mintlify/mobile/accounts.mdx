The **Accounts** tab lists every account linked to your organisation.

## Viewing your accounts

Each account appears as a card showing:

- The account name and the last four digits of the account number
- The **Available balance** in large type, with the **Book balance** underneath
- The account currency (for example USD or CDF)

Tap the eye icon on a card to hide or show its balance.

## Viewing account activity

Select **Tap to view activity** under an account to open its transaction history.

Use the tabs to filter the list:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every transaction on the account |
| **Credit** | Money received |
| **Debit** | Money sent or charged |
| **Pending** | Transactions not yet posted |

Use the filter icon on the right for more options.

<Tip>
To get a formal statement for a date range, use [Statements](/mobile/more#statements).
</Tip>
