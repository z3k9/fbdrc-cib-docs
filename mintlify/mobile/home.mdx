Home is the first screen you see after signing in. It gives you a quick read of your organisation's position and what needs your attention.

## What's on Home

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Greeting** | Your name, your role (for example `INITIATOR_AUTHORIZER`), and your organisation. The bell at the top right opens notifications. |
| **Account cards** | One card per account with its **Available balance**, **Book balance**, and currency. Swipe left or right to move between accounts. Tap the eye icon to hide or show the balance. |
| **Quick actions** | Shortcuts to **Send**, **Pay bills**, **Statements**, and **Beneficiaries** |
| **FX rates (live)** | Current rates for EUR/CDF, GBP/CDF, ZAR/CDF, and USD/CDF, with the time of the last update |
| **Pending approvals** | Items waiting for approval. Select **View all** to open [Approvals](/mobile/approvals). |
| **Recent transactions** | Your latest debits and credits. Select **See all** for the full list. |

<Tip>
Hide your balances before you use the app in a public place.
</Tip>
