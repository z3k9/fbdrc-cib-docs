First Bank DRC's Corporate Internet Banking (CIB) platform gives your business secure, self-service access to accounts, payments, collections, and administrative tools. You can use it through three channels, depending on your role and how you work.

## The three channels

| Channel | Who it's for | How you access it |
|---|---|---|
| **Web Portal** | Corporate officers: Clerks, Initiators, and Authorizers. The full set of features. | Web browser |
| **Mobile app** | Corporate officers who need balances, transfers, and approvals on the go | iOS and Android app |
| **Host-to-Host (H2H)** | Organisations sending high volumes of payments straight from their ERP or treasury system | Encrypted SFTP file exchange |

## How approvals work on every channel

CIB separates the people who **create** payments from the people who **approve** them:

1. An officer with the **Initiator** role submits a payment or request.
2. It waits in the **Transaction Queue** (payments) or **Activities Queue** (administrative changes).
3. An officer with the **Authorizer** role approves or declines it.
4. Approved payments are processed by the bank.

See [Roles and permissions](/web-portal/administration/roles-permissions) for details.
