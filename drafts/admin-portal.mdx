<Info>
The Admin Portal is used exclusively by **First Bank DRC staff** to manage customer onboarding and back-office operations. Corporate clients do not have access to this channel.
</Info>

<Warning>
This page is drafted from the original manual's "Back Office" module references and has **not** been validated against the actual staff-facing flow. Please review and correct.
</Warning>

## Reviewing New Enrollments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In">
    Log in to the Admin Portal with your staff credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to New Enrollments">
    Go to the **New Enrollments** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Submitted Documents">
    Review submitted Company Documents and enrolment details for each pending customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or Reject">
    Approve or reject the enrollment based on KYC compliance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Queues & Alerts

Monitor pending customer actions and system alerts requiring staff attention — for example, approvals awaiting sign-off, or flagged transactions.

## User Management

View and manage customer officer accounts, including approving newly created Initiators/Authorizers before they can log in.
